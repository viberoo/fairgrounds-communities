Protecting Your Home in Freezing Weather

Freezing temperatures can cause pipes to freeze and burst if proper precautions are not taken. To protect your home and prevent potential damage, please follow the instructions below:

Drip Indoor Faucets: Turn on both hot and cold water faucets in your kitchen and bathrooms. Set them to a slow, steady drip — the drip should be large enough to prevent water from standing still in the pipes but not wasteful.

Open Cabinet Doors: Open cabinet doors under sinks in the kitchen and bathrooms to allow warm air to circulate around the pipes, especially if the sink is on an exterior wall.

Keep Thermostat Above 65°F: Ensure your heating system is functioning and keep it set at no lower than 65°F, even if you are leaving for an extended period.

Outdoor Faucets and Hoses: Disconnect and store any outdoor hoses. If your outdoor faucets have covers, ensure they are securely in place.

Taking these steps helps prevent frozen pipes, which can lead to costly damages and repairs. If you notice any issues, such as a lack of running water or suspected pipe damage, please notify us immediately at 405-800-0137 or mail@fairgroundscommunities.com. Thank you for your attention to this important matter. Please stay warm and safe!
