Before You Apply

The application asks for your contact details, residency history, employment, vehicles, references, and a copy of your driver’s license. A background check requires submitting your Social Security number, and a pet fee and pet rent apply where pets are allowed (limit of 2 pets, dogs under 40 pounds only).
